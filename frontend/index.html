Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How accurate is the live stock?
Our stock levels update in real-time. When you place an order, items are immediately deducted from inventory so you always get what you ordered.
Can I order custom sizes for curtains or bedsheets?
Absolutely! Use our Request Quote page to describe your custom size, fabric preference, or design. Our team will respond with pricing and availability.
How do I know my order went through?
After placing your order you'll instantly see an Order Confirmation Number on screen. Our team processes it from Pending to Confirmed and will reach out if needed.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns on unused items in good condition. Those in good shape are restocked; damaged items are safely removed from active inventory.
Do you accept bulk or wholesale orders?
Yes! We welcome bulk orders for bedsheets, blankets, and curtains. Use the Request Quote page and specify the quantity. We'll send a special bulk pricing quote.
